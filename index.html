Our Story

Science Is Our
Foundation

SCI Physical Academy was born from a simple but powerful conviction: that every training decision should be grounded in scientific data, not guesswork.

Founded in Gerakas in 2019, we spent five years building a reputation for methodical, results-driven training. The demand grew — and so did we. Our second space in Paiania brings the same scientific approach to the wider Mesogeia region.
